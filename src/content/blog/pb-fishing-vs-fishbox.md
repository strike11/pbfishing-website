Fishbox shows up all over Reddit and in app ads, so a lot of anglers end up comparing it with whatever app they already use. With PB Fishing, the comparison is less head-to-head than it looks. Fishbox is a planning tool: maps, forecasts, baits, rigging and a pro you can message. PB Fishing is a record of what you caught, with your personal bests and state rankings on top.

We build PB Fishing. That's why the page leads with the verdict and spells out where Fishbox does more. We also tell you plainly which Fishbox prices we couldn't confirm.

## The 30-second verdict

- **Pick Fishbox** if you want depth and satellite maps, tides, species bite times, rigging guides and live chat with fishing pros in one app.
- **Pick PB Fishing** if you want a free log with no catch limit, PBs that update on their own, and a leaderboard for your state.
- **Use both** if you plan trips in Fishbox and log the results in PB Fishing. The log side is free.

## Logging catches

This is PB Fishing's core and only a side feature in Fishbox.

PB Fishing: snap a side-profile photo, the app suggests the species and estimates length, and you confirm or correct it. Date, water, weather and pressure attach automatically. Add weight if you weighed the fish; if not, the [fish weight calculator](/tools/fish-weight-calculator) estimates it from length. Unlimited logging is free, and catches made with no signal save on the phone and upload later.

Fishbox's App Store listing describes photo fish ID down to subspecies and a personal gallery. It doesn't describe a logbook with length, weight, conditions or catch history the way a dedicated log does. If keeping a record is the point, see [what to record in a fishing log](/blog/fishing-log-what-to-record) and [best fishing log apps](/blog/best-fishing-log-apps).

## PBs and leaderboards

PB Fishing keeps your best fish per species and updates it whenever you beat it. Each species has a state board ("Local") and a national board, reset every six months so new anglers have a real shot. An entry shows angler name, state, length and weight, with a photo behind it. That's photo-backed, not tournament-judged. The free tier shows the top 10; Premium shows the full national board.

Fishbox's listing doesn't mention personal bests or leaderboards. That's not a knock; it's simply not what the app is for.

## Maps

Fishbox wins this one. It lists bathymetric and satellite maps covering 50,000+ US lakes, rivers, reservoirs and coastal areas, with filters for species, depth, structure and access type. You can save unlimited favorites and download map tiles for water with no cell service.

PB Fishing covers 20,000+ named US waters. Each water page has species, seasonal activity, community ratings, live conditions and a bite forecast, plus a trip planner and links to the state's official license and regulation pages. No depth contours. If you fish structure, Fishbox's maps or a Navionics chart will do more for you.

## Forecasts

Fishbox's forecast list is long: wind, barometric pressure, temperature, rain, solunar times and tide charts, plus bite-time predictions by species and push alerts when conditions turn good. It also has seasonal calendars and an in-app assistant you can ask questions like what bait to throw for spring largemouth.

PB Fishing gives every water a daily bite forecast for free. Premium adds an hourly forecast with odds by species and 10-day weather. Neither app can promise fish. Use any bite score as one input; [barometric pressure and fishing](/blog/barometric-pressure-and-fishing) explains how much weight to give it.

## Community and expert help

Fishbox's take on community is access to people who know: 24/7 live chat with fishing professionals, video and photo tutorials on rigging and casting, and knot guides (Palomar, improved clinch, uni). For a beginner, a pro on call is a real advantage.

PB Fishing has a social side instead: a feed (For You and Following), 24-hour stories, likes and comments, plus the leaderboards. It's a young community. If you'd rather ask a pro than browse other anglers' catches, Fishbox fits better.

## Privacy

- **Fishbox's** listing describes favorites, a personal photo gallery and pro chat, not a public catch feed or catch map. Your saved spots aren't described as shared.
- **PB Fishing** leaderboard entries show your state, never the lake or coordinates. Feed posts are separate, so check your photo backgrounds and captions before you post.

## Price

PB Fishing Premium is $3.99 a month or $29.99 a year (about $2.50 a month), with a 7-day trial on yearly. The free tier includes unlimited logging, 5 photo IDs, a top-10 leaderboard preview, the daily bite forecast and up to 2 planned trips.

Fishbox is harder to pin down, and we'd rather say so than guess. As of November 2026:

- The US App Store's in-app purchase list shows ten Fishbox Premium items: $7.99, $9.99, $11.99, $19.99, $35.99 and five at $27.99. It doesn't say which is monthly and which is yearly.
- Google Play shows in-app items from $5.99 to $79.99.
- The listing says Premium comes yearly or monthly and that price may vary by location.
- Fishbox's website was running an 80%-off web offer when we checked, with no base price shown.

Some reviews put Fishbox at about $30 a year, but we couldn't confirm that on a store page. Look at the paywall screen in the app for the exact price before you start a trial.

<p class="callout warn"><strong>Check first:</strong> billing is the complaint you'll see most in Fishbox reviews; one 1-star reviewer said there was "no way of cancelling the trial other than contacting Fishbox directly." Subscribe inside the app on your phone and you can cancel through Apple or Google like any other subscription.</p>

PB Fishing bills only through Apple on iPhone (Settings, your name, Subscriptions) and Google Play on Android (Play Store, profile, Payments & subscriptions, Subscriptions).

## Where Fishbox is better

- **Maps.** 50,000+ US waters with depth, satellite imagery and filters. PB Fishing has no depth charts.
- **Offline maps.** Downloadable tiles for remote water.
- **Saltwater and tides.** Tide charts and coastal coverage; PB Fishing is built for US inland waters.
- **Expert help.** Live pro chat, rigging and knot guides, video tutorials.
- **Apple Watch.** Fishbox lists Apple Watch support; PB Fishing's listing is iPhone only on the Apple side.
- **Track record and reach.** Over a decade on the App Store and 1M+ installs on Google Play. PB Fishing's Android app launched in November 2026.

## Where PB Fishing is better

- **A real catch log**, free and unlimited, with photo, conditions and offline saving.
- **Automatic PBs per species.**
- **State and national leaderboards** that show your state, not your lake.
- **A clear price**: $29.99 a year or $3.99 a month, billed by Apple or Google Play.

Fishbox is the better planning tool, especially if you want depth maps and a pro to ask.
