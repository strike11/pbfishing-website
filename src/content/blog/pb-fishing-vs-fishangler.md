FishAngler and PB Fishing get compared for one reason: both give away a lot before asking for money. When someone on Reddit asks for a free Fishbrain replacement, FishAngler is usually the first name back. One r/bassfishing angler summed it up as "Good free features, isn't obtrusive with its paywall." That's a fair description, and we won't argue with it.

The difference is what each app is built around. FishAngler is a map and a community: where to fish, who's catching what, and what the weather is doing. PB Fishing is a log built around your personal bests and where they rank in your state. We build PB Fishing, so this page starts with the plain verdict and then shows where FishAngler is better.

## The 30-second verdict

- **Pick FishAngler** if you want the most you can get for $0 on the map side: ramps, piers, kayak launches, property lines, groups and local reports.
- **Pick PB Fishing** if you mostly fish the same handful of waters and care about your biggest bass, crappie or walleye, and how they stack up against anglers in your state.
- **Use both** if you scout with FishAngler and keep your PBs in PB Fishing. Both free tiers are real, so this costs nothing.

## Logging catches

FishAngler's logbook is deep. Its listing promises 45+ attributes per catch: species, bait, weather, GPS and more. If you like filling in every field, you'll like it. Every catch also feeds FINN, FishAngler's in-app guide that builds trip plans from your history; VIP comes with 500 credits a month for it.

PB Fishing goes the other way. You take a side-profile photo, the app suggests the species and estimates length from the photo, and you confirm or fix it. Date, water, weather and pressure attach on their own. Weight goes in if you had a scale. If you didn't, our [fish weight calculator](/tools/fish-weight-calculator) gives a fair estimate from length. Logging is unlimited on the free tier, and a catch made in a dead zone saves to your phone and uploads when you're back in range.

If you want a field journal, FishAngler gives you more fields. If you want to log a fish in ten seconds with the boat drifting, PB Fishing is quicker.

## PBs and leaderboards

This is where the two apps differ the most.

PB Fishing checks every catch against your best for that species and updates the PB on its own. Each species has a state board ("Local") and a national board. Seasons reset every six months, so an old fish doesn't sit on top forever. Every entry has a photo behind it. That makes it photo-backed, not tournament-judged; we explain the difference in [fishing leaderboard apps](/blog/fishing-leaderboard-apps). The free tier shows a top-10 preview; Premium shows the full national board.

FishAngler doesn't list species leaderboards on its App Store page. What it does have is built-in IGFA world records and US state records, handy when you want to know how far your fish is from the real record.

## Maps and finding water

FishAngler wins this section on the free tier. Its map covers lakes, rivers, inshore and offshore water, with access points, boat ramps, kayak launches, piers, marinas, reefs and shipwrecks. Property lines and land access data are free too, which matters a lot to bank anglers who don't want to trespass. VIP adds Garmin Navionics depth charts and 3D contour maps.

PB Fishing covers 20,000+ named US waters. Each water page lists species, seasonal activity, community ratings, live conditions and a bite forecast, with a trip planner and links to that state's official license and regulation pages. There are no depth charts. If you fish ledges and offshore structure, you'll need FishAngler VIP, Navionics or a sonar map card either way.

## Forecasts

Neither app can promise a bite, and neither should. Treat any score as a reference, not a guarantee; [best time to fish](/blog/best-time-to-fish) explains what actually moves fish.

FishAngler's free account gives a 7-day weather forecast with tide charts and moon phases. VIP adds FishForecast solunar bite windows by the hour, a 14-day forecast and animated radar, wind and current layers.

PB Fishing gives a daily bite forecast free for each water. Premium adds an hourly forecast with odds by species and a 10-day weather view.

## Community

FishAngler's community is far bigger. Google Play shows 1M+ installs, and its App Store listing has about 14,000 ratings. You get groups for bass, redfish, trout, carp and catfish, local water pages to follow, and reports from people fishing your area. One 5-star App Store reviewer likened it to Facebook for anglers, and meant it kindly.

PB Fishing's community is young. There's a feed (For You and Following), 24-hour stories, likes, comments and saves. On a lot of waters you'll find more local chatter on FishAngler today.

## Privacy: what other people see

- **FishAngler** sells exact catch locations in VIP: GPS coordinates of recent catches logged by anglers. It also offers private waypoints in VIP. Before you post a fish from a small pond, check how that catch is shared.
- **PB Fishing** leaderboard entries show your name, state, length and weight. They never show the lake or coordinates. The community feed is separate: what's in your photo background or caption is up to you.

On either app, the old habit still works best: crop out landmarks and don't name small waters.

## Where FishAngler is better

- **Free map features.** Ramps, piers, kayak access, property lines and structure at $0. PB Fishing has nothing like the property-line layer.
- **Community size and local reports.** More anglers means more reports from small waters.
- **Depth charts.** VIP includes Garmin Navionics and 3D contours. PB Fishing has no depth charts.
- **Saltwater.** Tides, offshore structure, FADs and oil platforms are on FishAngler's map. PB Fishing is built for US inland waters.
- **Free fish ID.** 866+ species on the free account, against 5 free IDs on PB Fishing.
- **Track record.** FishAngler has been on the App Store since 2016. PB Fishing is newer, and its Android app launched in November 2026.

## Where PB Fishing is better

- **Price.** $20 a year less than VIP, $3 a month less.
- **PBs as the main screen.** Your best fish per species, kept current without digging.
- **State and national leaderboards.** Six-month seasons, a photo behind every entry, and your state shown instead of your lake.
- **Fast photo logging** that works with no signal.
