Ask a Michigan ice angler for the best lake and you'll get a county name first. That's not a dodge. In Michigan, where you fish decides when your shanty must come off, whether you can keep a walleye in March, and how many perch go in the bucket. So each entry below gives you the fish, the rule that matters most there, and the removal date for that water.

The lake picks lean on the DNR's own "spots to try" list for ice fishing, which names the species biologists expect through the ice. Every limit and date comes from the **2026 Michigan Fishing Regulations** (license year April 1, 2026 to March 31, 2027). For the open-water side of the same lakes, see [best fishing lakes in Michigan](/fishing/michigan/best-lakes).

Dates are midnight on the date shown unless noted, from the 2026 booklet's ice shanty rules. Ice can force removal earlier.

## 1. Saginaw Bay

Saginaw Bay is Michigan's big walleye-and-perch ice show. It's Lake Huron water, which means the Lower Peninsula Great Lakes walleye season applies: **open all year**, so there's no March 15 closer here. On the bay and the lower Saginaw River the daily limit is **8 walleye with a 13-inch minimum**, compared with 5 at 15 inches statewide. Perch are 25 a day with no size limit.

**Shanty date:** this one splits. Bay and Arenac counties are on the DNR's northern list, so shanties stay until **March 15, 2027**. On the thumb side (Huron and Tuscola counties), the southern Lower Peninsula date applies: **March 1, 2027**.

**Watch out:** this is Great Lakes ice. The DNR's ice guidance warns that current weakens ice and that ice with open water at the shoreline calls for extra caution. On the bay, wind can open cracks and move ice. Check local reports every morning, not once a season.

## 2. Houghton Lake

At 20,075 acres, Houghton is Michigan's largest inland lake, and the DNR describes it as shallow, weedy and "excellent" for both summer and winter fishing. Its ice list names **walleye, bluegill, crappie and sunfish**. Think weed edges and flats, not deep structure, and expect a crowd on weekends.

**Rules:** Houghton is one of the waters where you may keep **up to 5 northern pike of any size, with only 1 of 24 inches or more**, which makes it a good tip-up lake. Lower Peninsula inland walleye and pike possession closes **March 15**. Sunfish, crappie and bluegill are 25 combined. Roscommon County is on the northern list: shanties off by **March 15, 2027**.

## 3. Little Bay de Noc

Little Bay de Noc at Escanaba and Gladstone is the Upper Peninsula's walleye headliner and one of the DNR's named ice spots for **walleye and perch**. The DNR and local partners plow the boating access site at Kipling in winter, one of just over 25 plowed UP sites.

**Rules:** north of a line from the Peninsula Point Lighthouse to the mouth of the Bark River, your daily limit may include **no more than 1 walleye of 23 inches or more**. Upper Peninsula walleye possession runs **May 15 to March 15**, so late-ice walleye must go back after the 15th. Perch are 25. UP shanties come off by **March 31, 2027**.

## 4. Lake St. Clair

Lake St. Clair is the metro Detroit ice lake, and the DNR lists it for **yellow perch, bluegill and crappie** through the ice. It's shallow and close to millions of people, so on good ice it's busy.

**Rules worth knowing:** Lake St. Clair has its own shanty deadline, **before sunset on the first Sunday after Feb. 20**, which works out to **Feb. 21, 2027**, weeks ahead of the rest of the Lower Peninsula. It's also one of the few places you may **spear yellow perch** with a hand-propelled spear, bow or crossbow from Dec. 1 to March 15 (Macomb, St. Clair and Wayne counties). Walleye are open all year here; the daily limit is reset each May 1 and posted at Michigan.gov/Fishing. Up to 5 pike of 24 inches or more may be kept. The international boundary runs through the lake, and Ontario water needs an Ontario license.

## 5. Higgins Lake

Higgins sits next to Houghton and is its opposite: 10,186 acres and about 120 feet deep, clear and cold. The DNR lists it for **lake trout, smelt, lake whitefish and northern pike** through the ice, and notes elsewhere that perch and smelt numbers tend to alternate year to year. This is a lake for heavier jigging gear and a flasher, fishing deep breaks for trout.

**Rules:** Higgins is a **Type E trout lake**: open all year, 3 trout a day, 15-inch minimum for lake trout, brown and rainbow trout and splake. Whitefish and cisco are 5 combined on inland lakes, and smelt are 2 gallons. Roscommon County, so shanties off by **March 15, 2027**.

## 6. Burt and Mullett lakes

Burt Lake and its neighbor Mullett, connected by the Indian River in Cheboygan County, are both on the DNR's ice list for **yellow perch and walleye**. They're big, open lakes on the Inland Waterway, close to I-75, so they're an easy weekend from downstate.

**Rules:** statewide limits apply to perch (25) and walleye (5, 15-inch minimum), and Lower Peninsula inland walleye possession closes **March 15**. Cheboygan County is on the northern list: shanties off by **March 15, 2027**.

## 7. Lake Gogebic

Lake Gogebic in the western Upper Peninsula is the DNR's named spot for **yellow perch and walleye** in the far west, and it has its own perch rule to protect big fish: **25 perch a day, but no more than 5 of 12 inches or longer**. It's a lake where a jumbo perch is a realistic personal best.

**Rules:** Lake Gogebic is also on the list of waters where you may keep **up to 5 pike of any size, only 1 of 24 inches or more**. UP walleye possession closes March 15. It isn't one of the Michigan-Wisconsin boundary waters, so the general UP shanty date applies: **March 31, 2027**.

## 8. Black Lake

Black Lake, on the Cheboygan-Presque Isle line, is on the DNR's ice list for **walleye, yellow perch and lake sturgeon**, and it's the only place in Michigan where sturgeon may be speared. The **winter sturgeon spearing season requires pre-registration**, and any fish taken must be registered on-site with DNR staff right away. Details and dates are at Michigan.gov/Sturgeon.

**Rules:** Black Lake is one of the waters where up to 5 pike of any size may be kept, only 1 of 24 inches or more. Commercial minnow taking is banned on the lake and tributaries. Shanties off by **March 15, 2027**.

## 9. Munuscong Bay

Munuscong Bay, on the lower St. Marys River system in Chippewa County, is a DNR-listed spot for **yellow perch and walleye**. The DNR's winter plowing program includes Munuscong Lake, which saves a lot of shoveling at the ramp.

**Rules:** Upper Peninsula walleye and pike possession runs May 15 to March 15. Perch are 25. Shanties off by **March 31, 2027**.

## 10. Les Cheneaux Islands

The Les Cheneaux Islands off Mackinac County's Lake Huron shore are a maze of protected bays, and the DNR lists them for **yellow perch, northern pike and splake**. Splake, a lake trout and brook trout cross, are a classic Great Lakes ice target.

**Rules:** splake here follow the Great Lakes trout and salmon rules: open all year, 10-inch minimum, 5 fish combined with no more than 3 of one species. Upper Peninsula pike possession runs May 15 to March 15, with a 24-inch minimum and 2 a day statewide. Shanties off by **March 31, 2027**.

## The rules that apply everywhere

- **Lines:** no more than **3 lines per person, tip-ups included**, and no more than 6 hooks or lures across them. Every tip-up must carry your name and address in legible English.
- **Shanty ID:** your name and address, driver's license number or DNR Sportcard number on **all sides** in letters at least 2 inches tall, painted or fixed directly on the shanty, not on a board hung from it. Tents and pop-ups that come off at the end of each day need no ID.
- **Removal dates:** midnight March 1 (most of the Lower Peninsula), March 15 (33 northern Lower Peninsula counties, from Alcona and Bay to Roscommon and Wexford, plus Michigan-Wisconsin boundary waters), March 31 (the rest of the UP), and Lake St. Clair before sunset on the first Sunday after Feb. 20. After the date, shanties must come off at the end of each day, and unsafe ice can force removal earlier. The DNR has warned that owners of shanties that fall through can face up to 30 days in jail, a $500 fine, or both.
- **Spearing:** northern pike and muskie may be speared through the ice from Dec. 1 to March 15 with a hand-propelled spear only, except on designated trout waters, Michigan-Wisconsin boundary waters and a list of named lakes closed to pike and muskie spearing (p. 17 of the booklet).
- **License:** anyone 17 or older needs one. The winter Free Fishing Weekend is the Saturday and Sunday of Presidents Day weekend, Feb. 13 to 14, 2027. Prices and options are on [Michigan fishing license](/fishing/michigan/fishing-license).
- **Seasons:** Lower Peninsula inland walleye and pike close March 15; panfish and perch are open all year. The month-by-month view is on [Michigan fish species and seasons](/fishing/michigan/fish-species-seasons).

## How to pick

- **Walleye with a generous limit:** Saginaw Bay, and stay safe on Great Lakes ice.
- **Panfish for a family day:** Houghton Lake or Lake St. Clair.
- **Jumbo perch:** Lake Gogebic, Munuscong Bay or Les Cheneaux.
- **Something different:** Higgins for lake trout and smelt, Black Lake if you drew a sturgeon spot.
