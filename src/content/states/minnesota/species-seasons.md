The short answer: the 2026 Minnesota fishing opener for walleye and northern pike was **Saturday, May 9**, and the DNR has already published the next two: **May 15, 2027** and **May 13, 2028**. But "opener" only matters for some species. Panfish, perch and channel catfish never close, bass now have a catch-and-release season in every month, and several big border waters run on their own calendars. All dates below come from the 2026 Minnesota Fishing Regulations (effective March 1, 2026 through February 28, 2027).

## Opener dates, 2026 to 2028

| Year | Walleye and northern pike | Muskie | Stream trout (streams) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026 | May 9 | June 6 | April 11 |
| 2027 | May 15 | June 5 | April 10 |
| 2028 | May 13 | June 3 | April 8 |

Stream trout harvest now opens on the second Saturday in April every year (a 2026 change). The bass harvest opener depends on the zone: May 23, 2026 in the southern and north-central zones, May 9 in the northeast zone.

## Month-by-month calendar

| Month | What's biting | Where | Season notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| January | Walleye, perch, crappie, sunfish, pike, eelpout through the ice | Lake of the Woods, Upper Red, Mille Lacs, Winnibigoshish, metro lakes | Lake trout winter season opens Jan. 16, 2027 (Jan. 1 in the BWCAW); Take a Kid Ice Fishing Weekend Jan. 16-18, 2027 |
| February | Late-ice walleye, crappie, perch | Same; shallow bays on late ice | Inland walleye and pike close Feb. 28, 2027 |
| March | Panfish on late ice; Rainy River walleye (catch-and-release) | Rainy River, Four Mile Bay; southeast streams (catch-and-release trout) | Shelters off inland ice by March 1 (south) or March 15 (north), 2027 |
| April | Crappie and sunfish in warming bays; stream trout | Shallow dark-bottom bays; southeast trout streams | Stream trout harvest opens April 10, 2027; walleye closed inland |
| May | Walleye opener; pike; catch-and-release bass | Shallow shoals, river mouths, rocky shorelines | Walleye/pike open May 15, 2027; bass harvest opens May 23 (most zones) |
| June | Walleye on rock and weed edges; bass spawning; muskie opens | Mille Lacs, Leech, Vermilion, metro lakes | Muskie open June 5, 2027; Take a Kid Fishing Weekend (June 5-7 in 2026) |
| July | Walleye deeper; smallmouth on rock; bluegill and crappie on weed edges; catfish | Big lakes' mid-lake structure; Mississippi and St. Croix | Lake trout inland summer season ends Sept. 30 |
| August | Walleye at night and on deep structure; catfish; muskie | Mille Lacs, Vermilion, rivers | Flathead catfish open to Nov. 30 |
| September | Walleye and perch moving shallow as water cools; smallmouth schooling | Big lakes, Lake Superior streams | Smallmouth harvest ends Sept. 13 (south and north-central); Sept. 30 end of stream trout harvest outside the southeast |
| October | Big pike, walleye and muskie feeding before ice; salmon and steelhead runs | Mille Lacs, Leech, North Shore rivers | Lake Superior lake trout closes Oct. 3, 2027 (Oct. 4 in 2026) |
| November | Muskie last call; first ice in the north late in the month | Muskie lakes; small northern lakes | Muskie closes Nov. 30; dark house spearing opens Nov. 15 |
| December | First ice: crappie, sunfish, perch, walleye | Small shallow lakes first, then the big lakes | Mille Lacs winter regulations start Dec. 1; Lake Superior lake trout reopens Dec. 1 |

Timing of the bite is approximate and moves with ice-out and ice-up each year. For why temperature drives so much of this, see [water temperature and fish activity](/blog/water-temperature-and-fish-activity).

## Walleye

**Season:** May 9, 2026 to Feb. 28, 2027 on inland waters. **Limit:** 6 walleye and sauger combined, only 1 walleye over 20 inches in possession. **Spawn:** shortly after ice-out, on rock, gravel and in rivers, which is why the season is closed through spring.

Big-lake exceptions are the rule, not the exception: Leech allows 4 (1 over 20), Vermilion protects 20 to 26 inches, Winnibigoshish 18 to 23, Lake of the Woods 19.5 to 28 with no more than 4 walleye. Conservation-license holders may not keep walleye over 20 inches. More on the lakes: [best fishing lakes in Minnesota](/fishing/minnesota/best-lakes) and our [walleye species guide](/species/walleye).

## Northern pike

**Season:** May 9, 2026 to Feb. 28, 2027. **Limits by zone:**

- **Northeast** (north and east of U.S. 53 from Duluth to International Falls): 2, all 30 to 40 inches released, only 1 over 40.
- **North-central:** 10, all 22 to 26 inches released, not more than 2 over 26.
- **Southern** (south of a line from Ortonville through Hutchinson, Glencoe and Chaska to the Mississippi): 2, minimum 24 inches.

The statewide possession maximum is 10, and zone limits apply where the fish was caught. Pike spawn in flooded marshes right after ice-out. Through the ice, they're the classic tip-up fish; dark house spearing for pike runs Nov. 15, 2026 to Feb. 28, 2027 with its own limits.

## Muskellunge

**Season:** June 6 to Nov. 30 (2026); June 5 opener in 2027. **Limit:** 1, minimum 54 inches inland (tiger muskie: 40 inches). Canada and Wisconsin border waters use a 50-inch minimum and open later (June 20 on Canada waters, May 23 on Wisconsin waters). Muskie spawn in May, which is why the season opens a month after walleye. Fall is trophy time: the certified state record (55 lb 14 oz) was caught on Mille Lacs on November 22, 2021.

## Largemouth and smallmouth bass

**Southern and north-central zones:** catch-and-release March 1 to May 22; harvest May 23 onward (largemouth through Feb. 28, 2027; smallmouth through Sept. 13, then catch-and-release). **Northeast zone:** catch-and-release March 1 to May 8, harvest from May 9. **Limit:** 6 combined. Bass spawn in late May and June when shallow water warms into the 60s °F. Full detail in the [Minnesota bass fishing guide](/fishing/minnesota/bass-fishing).

## Crappie

**Season:** continuous. **Limit:** 10 combined black and white crappie (5 on Leech and many other special-regulation lakes, some with a 10 or 11-inch minimum). Crappie spawn in May and early June in shallow bays, and they're the most popular early-ice and late-ice target. A new certified black crappie record, 4 lb 1 oz from Cedar Lake in Rice County, was caught May 3, 2026. See the [crappie guide](/species/crappie).

## Sunfish (bluegill and relatives)

**Season:** continuous. **Limit:** 20 combined statewide, but many lakes are now 5 or 10 (Leech, Winnibigoshish and the Cass chain are 5). Bluegill spawn in June in shallow colonies; that's when kids catch them by the bucket, and also why lakes are cutting limits. A 2 lb 3 oz bluegill from Big Stone Lake (July 7, 2026) is the new certified record. See the [bluegill guide](/species/bluegill).

## Yellow perch

**Season:** continuous. **Limit:** 20 daily, 40 in possession (the only common species where daily and possession differ). Perch spawn just after ice-out. Winnibigoshish and Lake of the Woods are the big perch lakes, and perch carry a lot of Minnesota ice fishing in late winter.

## Lake trout

**Inland summer:** May 9 to Sept. 30, limit 2. **Inland winter:** Jan. 16 to March 31, 2027 for lakes outside the BWCAW; Jan. 1 to March 31 for lakes entirely inside it. **Lake Superior:** Dec. 1, 2026 to Oct. 3, 2027, limit 3. Lake trout spawn in fall on rocky reefs. A trout/salmon stamp is required on designated trout lakes and Lake Superior for anglers 18 to 64.

## Stream trout (brook, brown, rainbow, splake)

**Streams statewide:** April 11 to Sept. 30, 2026, limit 5 combined, only 1 over 16 inches. **Southeast Minnesota streams** (Houston, Fillmore, Mower, Dodge, Olmsted, Winona, Wabasha, Dakota, Goodhue counties), 2026: catch-and-release Jan. 1 to April 10; harvest April 11 to Sept. 14; catch-and-release Sept. 15 to Oct. 15; closed Oct. 16 to Dec. 31. In 2027 the early catch-and-release window runs Jan. 1 to April 9. **Stream trout lakes:** summer May 9 to Oct. 31, winter Jan. 16 to March 31, 2027. Only 1 line on designated trout waters, and no live minnows on designated trout lakes. See the [rainbow trout guide](/species/rainbow-trout).

## Catfish

**Channel catfish:** continuous, limit 5, only 1 over 24 inches. **Flathead catfish:** April 1 to Nov. 30, limit 2, only 1 over 24 inches. Both spawn in early summer. The Mississippi, Minnesota and St. Croix rivers are the main catfish water; see the [channel catfish guide](/species/channel-catfish).

## Lake sturgeon

**Inland:** catch-and-release only, June 16 to April 14 every year, no tag needed. **Canada border waters (Lake of the Woods, Rainy River):** harvest April 24 to May 7 and July 1 to Sept. 30, 1 per calendar year, 45 to 50 inches or over 75, with a $5 sturgeon tag; closed May 16 to June 30; catch-and-release the rest of the year.

## Burbot (eelpout) and tullibee

**Burbot:** continuous, limit 4. A winter fish, best at night through the ice on Lake of the Woods, which produced the 19 lb 10 oz certified record in December 2016. **Cisco (tullibee):** continuous, 15 over 7 inches; a winter bonus on Mille Lacs.

## The ice season, in rules

- **Lines:** 2 through the ice (1 on designated trout lakes and streams); you must stay within sight of your lines and within 200 feet of a tip-up. New for 2026, non-motorized hook-setting devices are legal.
- **Shelters:** a license is needed for any shelter left unattended between midnight and 1 hour before sunrise, or when you're more than 200 feet away. Removal deadlines for inland waters are March 1, 2027 south of the U.S. 10 / Highway 34 / Highway 200 / U.S. 2 line and March 15, 2027 north of it; Canada border waters March 31.
- **Spearing:** dark house season Nov. 15, 2026 to Feb. 28, 2027 for pike, channel catfish and whitefish (flatheads only to Nov. 30).

<p class="callout warn"><strong>Ice safety:</strong> the DNR doesn't measure lake ice. Its guidelines for new clear ice are 4 inches for foot travel, 5 to 7 for a snowmobile, 9 to 10 for a small car; double them for white ice. Read the <a href="https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/safety/ice/thickness.html">DNR ice thickness guidelines</a>.</p>

## Closed seasons and sanctuaries to know

- Inland walleye, northern pike and muskie are closed between their season end and the next opener; fishing for them on purpose then is illegal.
- The St. Croix from the Taylors Falls dam to the Highway 8 bridge is closed to all fishing March 2 through June 15.
- No fishing within 300 feet below Lock and Dam 3 and Lock and Dam 4 on the Mississippi, March 1 to April 30.
- Paddlefish have no open season.
