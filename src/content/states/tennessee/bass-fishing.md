Bass fishing is the most popular kind of fishing in Tennessee, and TWRA puts the economic output of anglers in the state at about $1.2 billion a year. What makes it unusual is the range. On the same weekend you can throw a jerkbait for 4-pound smallmouth on clear Dale Hollow, drag a ½-ounce jig on a Kentucky Lake ledge, and fish hydrilla on Nickajack for a largemouth that might carry Florida genes. Each lake has its own rules, so this page pairs the pattern with the limit.

## The bass you will catch

| Species | Where | Record (TWRA) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Smallmouth | Dale Hollow, Norris, Center Hill, Pickwick, Watts Bar, Cherokee | 11 lb 15 oz, Dale Hollow, 1955 | Official state sport fish since 2005; TWRA says it is in most streams and lakes except West Tennessee, though Pickwick holds plenty |
| Largemouth (incl. Florida hybrids) | Chickamauga, Nickajack, Kentucky Lake, Old Hickory, Fort Loudoun | 15 lb 7.5 oz, Nickajack, 2026 | Florida stocking on Chickamauga since 2000, Nickajack since 2015 |
| Spotted (Kentucky) | Statewide reservoirs | 6 lb 1 oz, Chickamauga, 2011 | TWRA reports declines on Dale Hollow and Norris |
| Alabama | Parksville, Ocoee River and, per TWRA, numerous other reservoirs | 7 lb 3 oz, Ocoee River | Invasive here; do not move them |
| Coosa (redeye) | Parksville Reservoir | 1 lb 15 oz, Parksville, 1991 | Rarely targeted |

### Smallmouth

TWRA says Tennessee has produced the five largest smallmouth in the world. Dale Hollow is the headline: roving creel surveys show average smallmouth around 3 pounds and catch rates above average. Center Hill has similar rocky, bluff-lined habitat with above-average density. Pickwick produces trophy smallies that TWRA compares to Dale Hollow. Norris is best from November to April. TWRA says Watts Bar smallmouth responded well to an earlier 18-inch minimum and fish over 18 inches are common; note that the current guide lists a 15-inch minimum for all black bass there.

### Largemouth and the Florida-strain program

In 2000 TWRA began stocking Florida largemouth in Chickamauga to put bigger genes into the population. More than 3 million fingerlings later, the goal of 15% Florida genes was passed by 2010. Gabe Keen's 15-lb 3-oz record from February 13, 2015 was a 12-year-old F1 hybrid, and TWRA says those hybrids are the fastest-growing largemouth in the system. Nickajack started its own Florida stocking in 2015, and on February 28, 2026 Darren Nunley caught a 15-lb 7.5-oz largemouth there. TWRA certified it in May 2026 after genetic testing. Outdoor Life reported a length of 27 7/8 inches and said his guide had him tie on a ½-ounce green-pumpkin Z-Man JackHammer ChatterBait with a Hog Farmer Spunk Shad trailer. Watts Bar is also on the Florida bass stocking list.

### Spotted and Alabama bass

Spotted bass are common, with no length limit on most lakes, and often caught by smallmouth anglers on the same rocky banks. Alabama bass are a different story. TWRA calls them a new invader: populations explode, then crowd into lots of small fish, and they hybridize with smallmouth. TWRA has documented largemouth displacement at Parksville and smallmouth hybridization at Watts Bar. Never move a bass from one lake to another; at Parksville it is illegal to transport black bass alive away from the reservoir.

## The bass year in Tennessee

Water temperature runs the calendar more than the month does. These are the patterns TWRA's lake biologists describe, translated into a month plan.

| Month | Smallmouth | Largemouth | Where to start |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jan | Deep, slow; live shad, hair jigs | Schooled under shad on lower lake; jigging spoons, A-rigs | Norris, Dale Hollow, Kentucky Lake |
| Feb | Pre-spawn moves begin | Big females on warm, stained flats; both records came in February | Chickamauga, Nickajack |
| Mar | Shallow to points and gravel flats at 59 to 60 °F | Pre-spawn on grass and sloping banks | Pickwick, Dale Hollow |
| Apr | Spawn and post-spawn | Spawn peaks at 68 to 72 °F; spinnerbaits, plastics, square-bills | Old Hickory, Percy Priest |
| May | Topwater and swimbaits after the spawn | Post-spawn, Carolina rigs and lizards | Kentucky Lake, Pickwick |
| Jun | Night fishing begins | Ledges and grass near the channel | Kentucky Lake ledges, Old Hickory |
| Jul | Night jigs and spinnerbaits, 15 to 25 ft | Night bite when surface nears the low 80s | Dale Hollow, Pickwick |
| Aug | Same; shallow lakes slow down | Grass and ledges; Douglas above the thermocline | Douglas lower end, Kentucky Lake |
| Sep | Fish begin to follow shad | Same | Fort Loudoun, Watts Bar |
| Oct | Shad in creeks | Back into creeks on schooling shad; shad-colored baits | Cherokee, Chickamauga |
| Nov | Norris season opens up | Creeks, then main lake | Norris, Dale Hollow |
| Dec | Football jigs on points, 20 to 40 ft | Deep schools under shad | Dale Hollow, Center Hill |

## Lures that work here

These are straight from what TWRA lists for its own lakes, plus what the record fish ate:

- **Kentucky Lake and Pickwick ledges:** deep-diving crankbaits in blue and yellow or shad patterns (TWRA names the "Tennessee Shad" color), Alabama rigs, ½-ounce jigs, Carolina-rigged worms and lizards after the spawn.
- **Pickwick smallmouth in March:** lipless rattle baits, spinnerbaits and shad swimbaits; jigs with curly tails as water warms.
- **Dale Hollow:** crankbaits, jerkbaits and jigs that imitate crayfish, shad and alewives; topwater after the spawn; jigs and spinnerbaits at night in summer; live shad on circle hooks in winter.
- **Chickamauga and Nickajack:** spinners, topwater, lipless baits, swimbaits, blade baits and bladed jigs through grass; jigs in winter.
- **Winter everywhere:** jigging spoons and A-rigs over shad schools on the lower end; football jigs on tapering points.

## Regulations that catch people out

The statewide rule is 5 black bass a day, any combination, with no statewide length limit. Then almost every big reservoir overrides it:

| Water | Largemouth | Smallmouth | Spotted / Alabama |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dale Hollow | 15" min | 2/day, 16-21" protected slot (1 under 16, 1 over 21) | No length limit |
| Center Hill, Great Falls | 15" min | 18" min | No length limit |
| Chickamauga, Nickajack | 15" min | No min; only 1 smallmouth/spot/Alabama over 16" a day | Same |
| Kentucky Lake, Barkley | 15" min | 15" min | No length limit |
| Pickwick | 15" min | 15" min | No length limit |
| Old Hickory, Cheatham | 14" min | 18" min | No length limit |
| Percy Priest, Tims Ford, Normandy | 15" min | 18" min | No length limit |
| Norris | 14" min | Jun 1 to Oct 15: 1/day, 18" min; Oct 16 to May 31: 5/day, 15" min | No creel, no length |
| Cherokee | 15" min | Same seasonal rule as Norris | 15/day, no length |
| Douglas | Oct 1 to May 31 no length limit; Jun 1 to Sep 30 only 1 LMB or SMB over 16" | Same | No length limit |
| Watts Bar, Fort Loudoun, Tellico | 15" min | 15" min | 15" min (one black bass rule) |

Sources: TWRA lake pages and the 2026 Tennessee Fishing Guide region tables. Rivers have their own rules too: the Clinch from Hwy 25E to the state line has a 13 to 17 inch smallmouth slot, and the French Broad below Douglas has an 18-inch smallmouth minimum.

## What comes next for bass rules

TWRA presented a Black Bass Management Plan to the Fish and Wildlife Commission in August 2026 to guide bass management from 2027 to 2036, built with a stakeholder advisory committee. TWRA said it would finalize the plan in October 2026. Expect some of the limits above to change; check the current guide each spring.

## Trophy programs and proof

Tennessee has two ways to make a big fish official:

- **State record:** weigh on a certified, legal-for-trade scale with two witnesses, have a TWRA biologist identify the fish (no photo IDs), and file within 30 days. A new record must beat the old one by at least 1 ounce. Details on [Tennessee state record fish](/fishing/tennessee/state-record-fish).
- **Tennessee Angler Recognition Program (TARP):** minimum 22 inches for largemouth, 20 for smallmouth, 18 for spotted bass. Send a photo on a measuring board or a witness signature, $5 per fish, within 90 days. Five trophies earns a Master Angler patch.

If you released a big one without a scale, our [largemouth bass weight chart](/blog/largemouth-bass-weight-chart) and the [fish weight calculator](/tools/fish-weight-calculator) will put a number on it.
