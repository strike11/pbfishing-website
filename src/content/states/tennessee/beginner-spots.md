New anglers on r/FishingForBeginners ask the same question every week: how do I find a spot where I am allowed to fish, from the bank, close to home? In Tennessee the answer is better than in most states, because TWRA builds bank-fishing sites on the big Nashville lakes, runs a network of small Family Fishing Lakes with piers, and stocks trout into city ponds every winter. Kids under 13 fish free every day of the year. Here is where to go near each major city, and what to bring.

## Before you go: the three rules beginners miss

1. **Ages.** Under 13, no license. Ages 13 to 15, a $9 junior license. Adults 16 to 64 need at least the $33 Hunt/Fish Combo. Full detail on the [Tennessee fishing license](/fishing/tennessee/fishing-license) page.
2. **Lake permits.** TWRA Family Fishing Lakes need a $6 daily or $48 annual lake permit on top of the license for residents 16 to 64 and non-residents 16 and older (not at Garrett or Whiteville). Residents 65+ and anyone under 16 skip it. Buy online or in the TWRA On The Go app before you go, because Marrowbone has no concession.
3. **Trout.** Trout need the $21 trout supplemental unless you hold a Sportsman license or are under 13.

## Nashville

### Percy Priest Lake: Stewart Creek and Vivrette Creek bank sites
TWRA built two enhanced bank-fishing sites on J. Percy Priest. **Stewart Creek** in Smyrna has a full-size pier and five large platforms on a paved greenway tied into Smyrna's park system. **Vivrette Creek**, south of the Old Hickory Boulevard and Mt. Juliet exits on I-40, has a pier, a fishing jetty and four smaller platforms. Both have fish attractors sunk in casting range. Expect bluegill, crappie (best in early April) and largemouth.

### Old Hickory Lake piers
Old Hickory has eight public fishing piers, including Rockland Recreation Area, Sanders Ferry Park, Shutes Branch Recreation Area and the Shutes Branch city pier, Lock 4 Park at the mouth of East Station Camp Creek, and Little Cedar Creek Recreation Area. Crappie limit here is 30 a day at 10 inches.

### Below Percy Priest Dam, winter trout
Starting the first Friday in December, the Dale Hollow National Fish Hatchery stocks catchable rainbows below J. Percy Priest Dam, with more stockings in January and February at 2,000 trout each. TWRA calls it excellent winter bank fishing with high catch rates, in Metro Nashville. In April and May the same stretch of the Stones River fills with spawning white bass.

### Cedar Hill Park Pond (Madison) and Shelby Bottoms
Cedar Hill Park Pond is part of TWRA's Community Lakes Fishing Program, with winter trout stockings and a five-trout limit. Shelby Bottoms is also on the 2026-27 trout stocking schedule. Check the TWRA schedule for dates.

### Cheatham Lake catfish banks
West of the city on the Cumberland, TWRA names the Lock A and Pardue Recreation Areas and the bank below Old Hickory Dam as great places to catch catfish from shore. Cheatham has no fishing piers, so this is bank fishing at the public access sites.

### Marrowbone Lake (Joelton)
A 60-acre TWRA lake 15 miles north of Nashville with a pier and a ramp, trolling motors only. Bass limit is 10 a day with no length limit, so it is a good place to keep a couple of dinks for the pan. No concession: buy your lake permit online in advance.

## Memphis

### Cameron Brown Lake (Germantown)
Another Community Lakes Fishing Program site with winter rainbow stockings and a five-trout limit. Close, easy, good for kids.

### Shelby Farms
Shelby Farms is on TWRA's 2026-27 trout stocking schedule, and so is Don Fox Park. Check the schedule for which water and date.

### Glenn Springs Lake (Tipton County)
310 acres, 12 miles northeast of Millington, with fishing piers, ramps and fish attractors. From Memphis, take US 51 north to Millington. This is a TWRA family lake, so bring the lake permit; it closes Thanksgiving and Christmas.

### Bill Dance Lake State Fishing Area (Fayette County)
A 177-acre lake 15 miles north of Collierville off Fisherville Lake Road, with a pier, ramp, picnic areas and restrooms. Run by TWRA as a family fishing lake.

## Knoxville

### Fountain City Lake
A city lake in Knoxville in TWRA's Community Lakes Fishing Program, stocked with rainbows in winter. Five-trout limit, no size limit.

### Pistol Creek / Greenbelt Lake (Maryville)
Same program, in Maryville just south of Knoxville. Good for a short trip with small kids.

### Fort Loudoun Lake
14,600 acres near Knoxville, running 55 miles from the dam up to where the Holston and French Broad meet. TWRA notes quality largemouth and smallmouth, good enough to draw big tournaments. Shore access is wherever public land and ramps meet the water; check TDEC's consumption advisories before keeping catfish here.

## Chattanooga

### Camp Jordan (East Ridge)
Part of TWRA's Community Lakes Fishing Program in the 2025-26 season, with winter trout stockings and a five-trout limit.

### Harrison Bay State Park
Tennessee's 1,200-acre state park on Chickamauga Lake near Chattanooga has a fishing pier, accessible restrooms, a boat ramp and boat rentals, so you can fish from the pier or rent a boat for an hour.

### Below Chickamauga Dam and the Chickamauga creeks
In spring, TWRA says white bass concentrate on the banks below Chickamauga Dam and in North and South Chickamauga creeks, which is some of the best bank fishing around the city. Riprap banks on Nickajack also produce largemouth in spring.

## Family lakes worth a drive

| Lake | Where | Size | Why go |
|---|---|---|---|
| Williamsport Lakes | 10 mi NW of Columbia | 4 lakes, 13 to 80 acres | Whippoorwill Lake is youth (16 and under) with an adult only; bait, rentals, concession |
| Laurel Hill Lake | 15 mi W of Lawrenceburg | 325 acres | Youth-only fishing area by the office, pier, rentals, camping |
| Coy Gaither Bedford Lake | Near Shelbyville and Wartrace | 47 acres | Pier and fish attractors; trolling motors only |
| VFW Lake | 12 mi W of Lawrenceburg | 22 acres | Small and quiet, piers |
| Lake Graham | 9 mi E of Jackson | About 500 acres | Pier, attractors, birdwatching |

TWRA family lakes are open from half an hour before sunrise to half an hour after sunset (Garrett Lake is open 24 hours). Allowed gear is rod and reel, poles, handlines and jugs; no snagging.

## Free Fishing Day and kids' events

On Bobby Wilson Free Fishing Day, anyone can fish public waters, TWRA lakes and state park facilities without a license. TWRA lists the next one as Saturday, June 12, 2027, with kids 15 and under free all week through June 18. TWRA and local communities run kids' fishing events that day; TWRA's events calendar has the list. If your kid lands a first fish, TWRA will send a free signed **First Fish Award** certificate.

## Simple rigs that work here

- **Bluegill:** a slip bobber set 1 to 3 feet deep, a #6 or #8 long-shank hook and a cricket or piece of worm. This is exactly how Reelfoot regulars fish the cypress trees from May to July.
- **Crappie:** a 1/16 or 1/8 oz jig tipped with a minnow or a wax worm near brush, or a minnow under a bobber.
- **Winter trout:** a small inline spinner, or a hook with bait under a light bobber.
- **Below the dams:** TWRA suggests casting live shad or minnows on a #4 to 1/0 hook with a ½-ounce or heavier weight into the boils and eddies on the generator side.
