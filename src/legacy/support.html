Common questions

Frequently Asked

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your device, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, select PB FISHING, and choose to change or cancel your plan. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period.

How do I delete my account?

You can permanently delete your account from within the app: Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This removes your account and associated data and is irreversible.

I found a bug — what should I do?

Please email us at support@pbfishing.app with a description, your device and app version, and a screenshot if possible. Bug reports help us improve the app for everyone.

How is my data handled?

We never sell your personal information. Read the full Privacy Policy for details on what we collect and how it's protected.
