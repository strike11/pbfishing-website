## 3. Search research

**Minnesota opener 2027** (our biggest query family). Results are led by Explore Minnesota, the DNR seasons page, local TV, stateoutdoors.org. Two facts the ranking pages carry and ours doesn't:
- The 2027 **Governor's Fishing Opener is May 14–15, 2027 in Cold Spring** (Long Lake / Horseshoe Chain), the first time Cold Spring hosts. ([Explore Minnesota](https://www.exploreminnesota.com/governors-fishing-opener), [KTTC](https://www.kttc.com/2026/06/25/cold-springs-host-2027-governors-fishing-opener/))
- The DNR is moving to **cut the statewide walleye limit from 6 to 4 effective March 1, 2027**, pending an administrative-law-judge hearing; comments ran 53% for, 32% against. ([DNR proposal](https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/fisheries/management/walleye-proposal.html), [DNR news release](https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/news/2026/01/12/minnesota-dnr-proposes-reduce-statewide-walleye-limit-6-4)). Final status needs checking on the DNR page before we state it as law.

**90/10 rule** results: Salt Strong (podcast and articles), Wired2Fish, Western Bass, bassresource forum. Most are inshore-saltwater framed; our post is the freshwater/bass take, which is a gap we already fill. CTR fix is the title.

**"Why can't I catch any fish"**: half the results are video-game forums (Stardew Valley, itch.io). Real competitors: Iowa DNR "9 common fishing mistakes", Guidesly, Take Me Fishing. Our title already matches; a clearer "12 fixes" promise in the snippet should win clicks.

**"Best fishing apps 2026"** (Google): FishingBooker, Guidesly, FlyFisherPro, Hook & Barrel, onX (bass), Tom's Guide, Bass Pro 1Source, Wired2Fish, West Marine, Boatsetter. Most lists are generic and several are dated 2024; FishAngler, Fishbrain and Navionics are the consensus picks. None mention PB Fishing. Being added to these lists (outreach) is worth more than another listicle of our own.

**Seasonality** (Google Trends, US, 5 years):
- "fishing license": low now (18), climbs from April, peaks late June/early July (83–100).
- **"ice fishing": rising now, peaks early February** (2026 peak 38, twice "fishing license" in winter).
- "fishing app": small but growing every year; 2026 peak (8 in July) is 3–4× 2023–24.
- Autocomplete: "ice fishing app" is polluted by an unrelated gambling game, so target "ice fishing for beginners", "ice fishing minnesota lakes", "how thick does ice need to be", "ice fishing gear checklist" instead.
