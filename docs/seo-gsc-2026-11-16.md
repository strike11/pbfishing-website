## What we did about it (16 Nov)
1. **Minnesota opener 2027:** a dedicated page, `/blog/minnesota-fishing-opener-2027`, because the opener queries already rank 6–9 off a general seasons page. The seasons page links to it.
2. **License cluster (largest, worst position):** a 50-state hub at `/fishing-license` linking every agency and the five full license guides, plus links to it from the license pages and the catch-and-release post.
3. **Apps cluster:** new Android and iPhone landing pages (`/android`, `/iphone`), four new app listicles with PB Fishing at #1, four 1:1 comparisons, and the existing listicles reordered with PB Fishing first.
4. **Brand:** Organization/MobileApplication structured data lists both store URLs. The bigger lever is off-site (see below).

## Recommended, needs the owner
- Set the website field to https://pbfishing.app in App Store Connect (App Information → Marketing URL / Support URL) and in Play Console (Store settings → Store listing contact details → Website). Store listings linking to the site is the strongest brand signal for "pb fishing".
- Ask Obalus to link to https://pbfishing.app (not only the App Store) from their site.
