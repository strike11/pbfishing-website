Section 02

Information We Collect

2.1 Account & Profile Information

When you create an account we collect:

  • Email address (required for authentication)
  • Display name or username (required)
  • State, target species, experience level, fishing style, and fishing goals you choose during onboarding or profile setup
  • Profile photo (optional — chosen by you)
  • Subscription or membership status (to unlock premium features)

2.2 Catch Logs & Fishing Data

The core purpose of PB FISHING is to help you track your catches and personal bests. When you log a catch, we collect:

  • Catch details you enter: species, weight, length, date, and notes
  • Catch photos you upload for your records and, when you choose the Scan feature, AI species identification
  • Optional catch coordinates and a snapshot of weather and fishing conditions associated with the catch
  • Planned trips, including water body, date, target species, notes, and completion results
  • Your personal best (PB) records derived from your catch history

2.3 Location Data (Optional)

If you choose to use location-based features (e.g., recording a fishing spot, viewing nearby water conditions), the App requests access to your device location.

  • The App requests When In Use location permission only.
  • Your current coordinates are sent to our backend and weather provider when needed to return nearby waters, weather, marine conditions, and bite forecasts. PB FISHING does not maintain continuous background-location history.
  • If you save a catch with location enabled, its coordinates are retained with that private catch log. They are not shown as precise coordinates on leaderboards or Community posts, but de-identified and aggregated catch-location data may contribute to hotspot and activity scores.
  • You can turn location off at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services, or in Android Settings → Location → App permissions.

2.4 Camera & Photos (Optional)

  • Camera access — for taking catch photos directly in the App.
  • Photo library access — for selecting photos for catch logs, AI scans, avatars, or Community posts.
  • We do not access or upload photos you do not explicitly select or capture for an App feature.
  • Community images are screened on-device for explicit content before upload. The on-device screening result is not sent to a third-party image-moderation service.

2.5 Notifications (Optional)

  • PB FISHING uses local notifications only — scheduled on your device — to deliver reminders, condition alerts, personal-best alerts, and trip-day countdowns you have enabled.
  • No device push token is collected or transmitted to our servers.
  • Likes, comments, and follows may create in-app activity notifications stored in our backend; these do not generate remote push notifications.
  • You can manage notification preferences in the App (Settings → Notifications), in iPhone Settings → Notifications → PB FISHING, or in Android Settings → Apps → PB Fishing → Notifications.

2.6 In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

  • Subscription entitlement status (active / inactive) — to gate premium features.
  • Apple transaction identifiers, or Google Play purchase tokens, and verification results used to validate entitlement and prevent subscription fraud.
  • On iPhone, payment processing is handled by Apple via StoreKit. On Android, it is handled by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your payment card details.

2.7 Community Content (Optional)

If you participate in community features, we process:

  • Posts, photos, captions, comments, likes, saves, follows, collaborator tags, and water-body tags you create
  • Reports you submit, moderation violations, and temporary or permanent Community restrictions
  • Leaderboard rankings and aggregate hotspot/activity scores derived from catch data
  • Your display name, avatar, state, follower counts, Community content, and leaderboard records may be visible to other signed-in users

2.8 Technical & Usage Data

When the App communicates with our providers, they may automatically process limited request and security data, such as:

  • IP address, request timestamps, authentication/session identifiers, and basic client or app information included in network requests
  • Diagnostic errors and server logs needed to secure, troubleshoot, and operate the Service
  • AI scan usage counts used to enforce free-tier limits

We do not use advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs, and we do not sell or share data for cross-context behavioral advertising. On iPhone we do not request App Tracking Transparency permission. On Android we do not use the Advertising ID.

Section 05

Security

5.1 Encryption in Transit

All network communication between the App and our backend is protected with industry-standard transport-layer encryption (HTTPS/TLS). We enforce HTTPS-only connections.

5.2 Access Controls

We use authentication mechanisms and row-level database security rules to restrict private account data. Administrative access is limited to authorized personnel and service providers who need it to operate, secure, or support the Service.

5.3 Data Minimization

We aim to collect only the data necessary to provide the features you use. We do not build advertising profiles and do not aggregate your data for sale.

No security system is perfect. Please use your judgment when sharing sensitive information and use a strong password for your account.
Section 06

Data Retention

  • Account, catch, and planned-trip data — retained while your account is active or until you delete the relevant record, subject to limited legal and backup retention.
  • Uploaded photos — catch photos are retained while attached to a catch; avatar and Community photos are retained until replaced, deleted, or the related account/content is deleted.
  • AI scan photos — PB FISHING does not intentionally save the scan image to its database or storage bucket. The image is processed by Supabase and OpenAI to return a result; OpenAI's default abuse-monitoring retention may be up to 30 days.
  • Current location requests — used to return nearby waters and conditions and cached briefly in memory; we do not maintain continuous background-location history. Coordinates saved with a catch remain with that catch until deletion.
  • Community safety records — reports, violations, and enforcement records may be kept as reasonably necessary to prevent abuse, resolve disputes, and protect users.
  • Notification preferences — stored locally on your device only; not collected by our servers.
  • Account deletion — when you delete your account in-app (Settings → Delete Account), we initiate deletion of your account and associated data. Residual copies may remain temporarily in backups, security logs, or records we must retain for legal, fraud-prevention, or safety purposes.
  • Legal & financial records — we may retain limited records required for legal compliance (e.g., transaction-related records) for the period required by applicable law, even after account deletion.
